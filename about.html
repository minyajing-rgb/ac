雪夜有光。
A light in the snow.

一座虚构的瑞士阿尔卑斯山礼拜堂，一段可在浏览器里独自探索的安静时刻。

A quiet, browser-based virtual chapel inspired by a stylized Swiss Alpine twilight.

开始探索 / Explore the chapel →

体验什么

环视雪夜外景，拉近发光的礼拜堂，进入烛光内景；伴随 Scott Buckley《First Snow》背景音乐与浏览器合成的互动提示音，点击召唤极光、流星雨，以及借用基督教圣灵白鸽意象的发光效果。无需注册，免费体验。

支持桌面和手机浏览器。鼠标拖动或手指滑动环视，滚轮或双指捏合缩放；点击画面聚焦。

What you can explore

Look around a snowy exterior, move closer to the lit chapel, and enter its candlelit interior panorama. Listen to Scott Buckley’s “First Snow” and synthesized interaction tones, then summon an aurora, a meteor shower, or a luminous dove inspired by Christian imagery of the Holy Spirit. Free to play, with no account required.

Use a mouse or touch to look around, a wheel or pinch gesture to zoom, and a click or tap to focus the scene.

创作说明

Alpine Chapel 是由 SAGA1001 制作的互动艺术原型。雪夜与室内画面是 AI 生成的艺术化概念场景，并非某座真实瑞士教堂的实景照片。当前版本使用 3D 全景空间与实时特效，尚非可自由行走的建筑模型、正式礼拜或圣经教学。

想探索我们的独立圣经项目，可访问 From John to Jesus。

About the project

Alpine Chapel is an interactive art prototype by SAGA1001. Its snowy exterior and interior are AI-generated concept scenes, not photographs of an actual Swiss church. The current release combines a 3D panorama with real-time effects; it is not a walkable architectural model, a worship service, or Bible teaching.

For a separate Bible-focused project, visit From John to Jesus.
